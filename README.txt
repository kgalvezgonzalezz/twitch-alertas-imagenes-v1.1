For this program to work correctly on your 
stream, just follow these 4 simple steps:

STEP 1: START THE PROGRAM
Double-click the "Iniciar_Programa.bat" file. 
- If it's your first time, the program will download and install all the necessary components by itself (just click "Yes" if Windows asks for administrator permissions). 
- Everything is automatic! You don't have to close or restart anything, just wait a few seconds until your Admin Panel opens in your browser. Remember to keep the black console window open and minimized whenever you stream.

STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR TWITCH CHANNEL
On the web page that just opened, you will see a section that says "Twitch Connection". Type your Twitch username there and click "Connect". You will see the status tag change to green. The program will save this in its memory so you never have to do it again! (if you want to connect to another twitch channel name, just delete the config.json file).

STEP 3: CONNECT WITH OBS
Go to your OBS Studio and create a new "Browser Source":
- URL: http://localhost:3000
- Width: 1920
- Height: 1080
- Custom CSS: Delete absolutely everything in that box and leave it completely blank!

STEP 4: READY TO MODERATE!
In your Admin Panel, click the "🔊 Enable sound notifications" button. Now, every time someone uses bits, subs, or channel points sending an image link, you will hear a sound alert and you'll be able to see the link and its file size before approving the image to your stream. 

💡 EXTRA TIPS:
- Security: Any file larger than 500MB will be automatically blocked and deleted by the server to protect your internet connection.
- Language: You have a button on the top right corner to switch the entire panel to English (EN) or Spanish (ES).
